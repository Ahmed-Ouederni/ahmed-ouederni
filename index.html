About

I am Ahmed Ouederni, future software and game developer from Tunisia. I make games, build desktop tools, write backend systems, and tinker with hardware when something catches my interest. I live in Linux day to day (Linux Mint, Fedora, Ubuntu and others), so I am comfortable in the terminal, installing, configuring and fixing my own setup.

I graduated secondary school ranking top 70 nationally. I am currently studying at university, working toward my goal of becoming a professional engineer. I have been building games for over 5 years, and I work across desktop, web, and backend - whatever the project demands.

Not locked into one stack. If something interests me, I learn it and I ship it. A lot of games shipped solo and counting.
